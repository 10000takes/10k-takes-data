Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

The 10K Takes Dashboard is an internal tool operated by 10K Media LLC ("we"). It is used only to read public follower counts for social media accounts that 10K Media LLC owns and operates.

What we access

When an account owner authorizes the app, it reads the account's display name and follower count. It does not read private messages, post content, or any information about the account's followers or other users.

What we store

We store the follower count and the date it was recorded. Authorization tokens are kept in a private secrets store and are used only by the app. We do not collect personal information about visitors or followers.

Sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties. Follower totals may appear on an internal dashboard.

Removing access

The account owner can revoke the app's access at any time in the platform's settings, or by contacting us. We will delete stored tokens on request.
